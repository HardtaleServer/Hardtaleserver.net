Admin
prefix.6.<gradient:#FF6A5B:#FFB057>[ADMIN] </gradient>

Moderator
prefix.5.<gradient:#63AFFF:#7A87FF>[MOD] </gradient>

Helper
prefix.4.<gradient:#57D493:#79C9B2>[HELPER] </gradient>

Mythic
prefix.3.<gradient:#FF621D:#66B6C9>[MYTHIC] </gradient>

Legend
prefix.2.<gradient:#FF9A3D:#8F79C9>[LEGEND] </gradient>

Hero
prefix.1.<gradient:#2CCF9E:#D95B54>[HERO] </gradient>

Linked
prefix.0.<#479284>🔗 [LINKED] </#479284>

Unlinked
prefix.-1.<#A9B3C7>🔓 [UNLINKED] </#A9B3C7>

msg.legend.<gradient:#FF9A3D:#8F79C9>{message}</gradient>
